How payment works

Purchases are paid by crypto - this lets us take payments reliably as a Lebanon-based team, since traditional card processors don't support merchants here. Never used crypto before? It's easier than it sounds:

  • Pick a pack below and click Buy - you'll get a secure payment page with a wallet address and QR code
  • Buy a small amount of USDT (a stablecoin worth $1, not volatile like Bitcoin) on an app like Binance, Coinbase, or Bybit, paying with your normal card
  • Send it to the address shown, or just scan the QR code from your exchange app
  • Credits land in your account automatically within a few minutes - no manual approval needed

It's a one-time, direct transfer - no card details are ever stored anywhere, and nothing is auto-charged again.
