Where do I find my team ID?
  1. Log into fantasy.premierleague.com in a browser and open "Points".
  2. The page's URL ends in something like /entry/1234567/event/9.
  3. That number (1234567) is your team ID. The FPL app doesn't show it.

No Team ID?

Tap your 15 players: 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders and 3 forwards. The most-picked players are listed first, or search by name.
